Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions to cancel or change your plan.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

Is my data synced across devices?

Yes! Sign in with the same account on all your devices and your tasks, habits, and focus sessions will sync automatically.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, Plan Todo works offline. Your data syncs when you reconnect to the internet.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
